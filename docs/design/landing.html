How it works

From a messy group chat to a settled trip, with the organizer approving every step that matters.

  1. 01

    Paste the group chat

    The assistant reads the amount, the people and the deadline, and quotes the line it used for each.

  2. 02

    Confirm the terms

    Nothing is active until you confirm. Code checks the math, not the model.

  3. 03

    Everyone holds their share

    Each friend opens a link on their phone and approves a PayPal hold. No card form.

  4. 04

    Kept or released

    Everyone in by the deadline: captured. Anyone short: every hold is voided and nobody is charged.

A hold is not a charge

The usual way to book a trip is one person paying for everyone and chasing the rest. A hold reverses that.

Pay now, sort it out later

  • Money leaves on day one
  • One person fronts the whole $320.00
  • If the trip falls through, everyone chases refunds

Hold now, decide at the deadline

  • Nothing is taken while the group is short
  • Each person is only ever on the hook for their own share
  • Not enough people: every hold is released and nobody pays
